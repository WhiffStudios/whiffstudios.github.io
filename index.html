About Whiff Studios

Whiff Studios is a small independent studio exploring the intersection of technology, game development, software quality, creativity, and practical problem solving.

We're bringing years of professional technology experience into something new: building games and applications from the ground up.

This site is where we'll share that journey as the studio, our projects, and our ideas develop.
